Q46. [EN | cover | easy]
Question     : How do I calculate nominal cover from minimum cover?
Ground truth : Nominal cover = minimum cover + execution tolerance allowance Δcdev (recommended 10 mm). Formula: cnom = cmin + Δcdev. Clauses 4.4.1.1 and 4.4.1.3.
GT clauses   : 4.4.1.1, 4.4.1.3
Model answer : The Eurocode defines the **nominal cover** as the **minimum (required) cover** increased by the allowance for execution tolerances.

* **Definition** – Clause 4.4.1.1 (2) states that the nominal cover is “the minimum cover (see 4.4.1.2) **plus a margin of calculation for execution tolerances** (see 4.4.1.3)”【Context 4】.  

* **Allowance for tolerances** – Clause 4.4.1.3 (1) requires that, for the calculation of the nominal cover, “the minimum cover must be **augmented** at the design stage to take account of the execution‑deviation tolerance (Δ). Thus the minimum cover is increased by the absolute value of the adopted deviation that could reduce it”【Context 2】.

**Therefore, the nominal cover (c_nom) is obtained by:**

\[
c_{\text{nom}} = c_{\text{min}} + |\Delta|
\]

where  
* \(c_{\text{min}}\) = minimum cover required by durability, exposure class, etc. (Clause 4.4.1.2), and  
* \(|\Delta|\) = absolute value of the execution‑deviation tolerance adopted for the project (Clause 4.4.1.3).  

This relationship follows directly from Clause 4.4.1.1 (2) together with the tolerance provision in Clause 4.4.1.3 (1).
