  **Minimum concrete cover (c)**  
  
  - EC2 4.4.1.2 (2) requires that the cover be the **largest value** needed to satisfy **all** of the following:  
  
    1. The **adhesion** requirement (minimum cover for bond).  
    2. The **environmental‑exposure** requirement (cover needed for corrosion protection).  
    3. The **safety‑margin** requirement.  
    4. Any **reductions** that may be applied (e.g., for stainless‑steel reinforcement or for additional protective measures).  
  
  - EC2 4.4.1.2 (3) adds that the cover **must not be less than the value given in Table 4.2**.  
  
  - The **environmental‑exposure** part is linked to the exposure classes defined in **Table 4.1**, which are applicable to lightweight‑aggregate concrete in the same way as to normal‑weight concrete (EC2 11.4.1 (1)).  
  
  **Thus, the minimum concrete cover depends on:**  
  
  1. **Bond (adhesion) requirements**,  
  2. **Exposure class / environmental conditions**,  
  3. **Prescribed safety margin**, and  
  4. **Any applicable reductions** (e.g., stainless steel or extra protection).  
  
  The governing clause is **EC2 4.4.1.2** (with reference to Table 4.2) and the exposure‑class definition in **EC2 11.4.1**.

  **Exposure classes** are the classifications of the physical‑chemical conditions to which a concrete element is subjected, used to define the durability requirements for that element.  They are defined in the “conditions d’environnement” and are listed in Table 4.1 of EN 1992‑1‑1, which is based on EN 206‑1 [Clause 4.2 (1‑2)].
  
  The standard groups the exposure classes into the following categories (as shown in Table E.1 N of Clause 12.9.3):
  
  | Category (type of attack) | Exposure‑class symbols |
  |----------------------------|------------------------|
  | **Corrosion induced by carbonation** | XC1, XC2, XC3, XC4 |
  | **Corrosion induced by chlorides (external source)** | XD1, XD2, XD3 |
  | **Corrosion induced by chlorides of seawater** | XS1, XS2, XS3 |
  | **Freeze‑thaw attack** | XF1, XF2, XF3 |
  | **Chemical attack** | XA1, XA2, XA3 |
  | **No risk of attack** | X0 |
  
  These symbols are the “classes indicatives de résistance” that correspond to the exposure categories and are used to select appropriate concrete strength and cover requirements [Clause 12.9.3 (Table E.1 N)].
