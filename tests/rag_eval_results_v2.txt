  The Eurocode requires that a **minimum concrete cover (enrobage minimal c)** be provided so that the reinforcement is protected and the bond between steel and concrete is reliable.  
  
  - The cover to be used is **the greatest value that satisfies both the bond (adhérence) requirements and the environmental‑exposure requirements**, but it may never be less than **10 mm**.  (per EC2 cl. 4.4.1.2 (2) – “= max{ …, 10 mm } …”)  
  
  - The required value is taken from **Table 4.2**, which gives the minimum cover for the different exposure classes.  (per EC2 cl. 4.4.1.2 (3) – “l’enrobage minimal ne soit pas inférieur à donné dans le Tableau 4.2.”)  
  
  - The final cover may be increased by a **safety margin** (cl. 4.4.1.2 (5)) and can be **reduced** when:  
    * the reinforcement is **stainless steel** (cl. 4.4.1.2 (6)), or  
    * **additional protective measures** are provided (cl. 4.4.1.2 (7)).  
  
  Thus, the minimum concrete cover depends on:  
  
  1. **Bond requirements** (adhérence),  
  2. **Exposure class / environmental conditions**,  
  3. **A minimum of 10 mm**,  
  4. **Any applicable safety margin**, and  
  5. **Material or protective‑system adjustments** (stainless steel or extra protection).  
  
  All of the above are stipulated in **Eurocode 2, Clause 4.4.1.2**.
