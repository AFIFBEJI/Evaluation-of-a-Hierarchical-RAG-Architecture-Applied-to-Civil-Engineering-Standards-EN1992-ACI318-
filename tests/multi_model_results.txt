  Q: How do I calculate nominal cover from minimum cover?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Answer (27.74s):
    Per EC2 clause 4.4.1.1(2), the nominal cover is defined as the minimum cover plus a calculation margin for execution tolerances. The calculation margin is specified in EC2 clause 4.4.1.3. According to EC2 clause 4.4.1.3(1), the minimum cover must be increased by the absolute value of the execution tolerance that can reduce it. The recommended value for this tolerance is 10 mm, as stated in the note to EC2 clause 4.4.1.3(1). Therefore, to calculate the nominal cover, you should add the minimum cover to the execution tolerance, which is typically 10 mm, unless a different value is specified in the National Annex.
