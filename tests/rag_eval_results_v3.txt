  **Calculation**
  
  The nominal cover is obtained by adding a margin for execution tolerances to the minimum (required) cover:
  
  \[
  \text{Nominal cover } (c_{\text{nom}})= c_{\text{min}} \;+\; \Delta c
  \]
  
  where  
  
  * \(c_{\text{min}}\) = minimum cover prescribed for durability (clause 4.4.1.2, referenced in 4.4.1.1)  
  * \(\Delta c\) = absolute value of the adopted execution deviation (tolerance) (clause 4.4.1.3).
  
  **Allowance for deviation**
  
  Clause 4.4.1.3 states that the minimum cover must be **increased by the absolute value of the adopted execution deviation** to obtain the nominal cover. The recommended default value for the deviation is **10 mm** (unless a national annex provides another value).  
  
  The clause also allows a reduction of this tolerance in special situations:
  
  * If a quality‑assured production system with precise measurement is used, the tolerance may be reduced to **5 mm** (clause 4.4.1.3, note).  
  * If very precise measuring equipment and rejection of non‑conforming elements (e.g., prefabricated elements) are guaranteed, the tolerance may be reduced to **0 mm** (clause 4.4.1.3, note).
  
  **References**
  
  * Definition of nominal cover = minimum cover + tolerance: EC2 clause 4.4.1.1 (2) and (5) (Context 2 & 5).  
  * Requirement to increase the minimum cover by the absolute value of the adopted deviation: EC2 clause 4.4.1.3 (1) (Context 1).  
  * Recommended default deviation = 10 mm and possible reductions to 5 mm or 0 mm: EC2 clause 4.4.1.3 (note) (Context 1).
